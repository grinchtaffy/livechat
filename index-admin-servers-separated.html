Please read before continuing

By continuing into this app, you consent to an admin being able to view your camera at any time.

Whenever an admin is actually viewing your camera, a red “🔴 LIVE — an admin is viewing your camera” banner will appear on your screen, so you always know when it's happening.

If you don't want this, don't continue — just leave. If you do continue, you can turn this off at any time in Settings → Allow admin live view.

🎙️ Call recording

This website may record your calls for feedback. Is that okay?

If you choose No, your calls will never be recorded. You can change this any time in Settings → Call recording.
